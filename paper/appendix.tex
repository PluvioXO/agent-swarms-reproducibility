\section{Study Inventory and Protocols}
\label{app:protocol}
This appendix documents the eleven complementary studies represented in the
normalized evidence export. It omits prospective scaling models, unfinished experiments, and
imported full-benchmark baselines. Study codes below identify the complete
cell table in the versioned repository and the machine-readable evidence bundle.
Counts are completed scientific trial keys, not request attempts.

\subsection{Design and selection}
The five original grids evaluate widths 1, 2, 4, 8, and 16 at three evaluation
ceilings, with distinct calibration tasks and shared single-worker controls.
Draft, revision, and model integration receive nominal shares of 50\%, 25\%,
and 25\%. The five smaller diagnostic studies compare independent one,
independent four, and ring four at two ceilings, using draft and revision
shares of 50\% each without a model integrator. Ring revision receives a
predecessor draft; independent revision receives the worker's own draft.
Prompt costs and output reserves can prevent calls from launching.

BC-L records \path{gpt-5.6-luna}; later Luna studies request
\path{openai/gpt-6-luna}; Muse records
\path{meta/muse-spark-1.3-contributor}. Models, routing, and sampling settings
vary by study. Recorded activation is available only for the four programming
studies. Exact grids, recorded configurations, task-source revisions,
excerpt limits, and available dependency records are in the repository's
Methods document and configuration export.

\subsection{Task construction and confirmation}
Graph colouring uses 100 fixed, locally generated greedy-solvable
three-colour graphs: 20 each at 10, 12, 14, 16, and 18 vertices, with edge
probability 0.2. Both models share these tasks, giving 1,200 evaluation
trials per model. This is a local adaptation inspired by Reasoning Gym,
not its official task distribution. Countdown uses 100 algorithmically
generated puzzles with four to eight supplied numbers. Both families have
deterministic correctness checks; valid solutions need not be unique.
BBEH uses 25 tasks from each of four categories; PlanBench uses 100
Blocksworld Hard instances and a local STRIPS interpreter. These subsets
are not full-benchmark or unaided-collaboration evaluations.

Original ZebraLogic compares calibration-selected width policies with its
selected fixed-width baseline. Confirmation transfers communicating widths
4/8/16 without refitting to 300 unused puzzles, with 12 per size stratum.
Its primary comparison uses communicating fixed four at the two higher
ceilings. Shared cells are deduplicated into eleven configurations and
6,600 trials. The complete grids and generator settings are in the repository.

\section{Resource Accounting, Failures, and Provenance}
\label{app:accounting}
Retained tokens combine reported usage, local estimates, and conservative
full-allocation debits for eligible unknown-usage calls, depending on the
study. They are not exact physical compute or complete attempted-call
expenditure. Comparisons use the same study and ceiling.

Twelve BC-M calibration trials exceed their nominal ceilings by at most
1.92\%. No retained evaluation ledger total exceeds its nominal ceiling;
that check does not establish that every provider call or unobserved attempt
respected its allocation. Seven Muse graph-colouring trials at 8,192 were
classified as budget-violation failures under a post-start amendment.
They remain operational zeros, not direct evidence of reasoning errors.
Actual total tokens remain unknown for both graph models, as does Muse's
total cost. Attempt totals, known costs, missing-usage counts, and the seven
failure keys are preserved in the repository's Data document and graph export.

PlanBench's independent four-worker cells retain 1,600 stage calls per
ceiling: 1,599 finish at the length limit at 8,192 and three at 16,384.
The single-worker cells retain 400 calls each without length-limit finishes.
These observations diagnose feasibility but do not isolate a causal effect
of truncation.

\paragraph{Amendments and exclusions.}
Original ZebraLogic and confirmation include post-start accounting or retry
amendments and cannot be described as uniformly preregistered. Pilots,
smoke checks, imported baselines, incomplete studies, and one superseded
MBPP+ Muse row are excluded. Pilot truncation warnings were acknowledged
before some frozen evaluations continued. Detailed operational records and
excluded-check counts are documented in the repository; retained outcomes
do not imply zero expenditure on failed attempts.

\paragraph{Analysis artifacts and availability.}
The versioned public repository provides all 26,648 normalized retained
trials, all 316 cell summaries, a readable table of all 176 evaluation cells,
extended protocols, recorded configurations, provenance hashes, and the
offline aggregate verifier.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/PluvioXO/agent-swarms-reproducibility/tree/reproducibility-v1.3}}
The verifier checks saved aggregates and point estimates; it does not
recompute intervals, validate graders, or replay inference. The shared graph
export includes task edges, witness colourings, and operational-failure keys.
The controlled-study note contains the supplied protocol and result summary
and states which supporting records are available. Its coverage is separate
from the eleven complementary studies checked by the offline verifier.
Full experimental source/run archives, historical reports, and raw provider payloads are
outside this concise public package.

\section{Paired Inference and Sensitivity Analyses}
\label{app:inference}
Tasks are the resampling clusters, retaining all their configurations and
repetitions so contrasts remain paired. Confirmation uses size strata;
diagnostic studies use graph size, Countdown size, BBEH category, or PlanBench
object count. Intervals are conditional and study-specific, with no pooled
meta-analysis or cross-study multiplicity correction. Resampling draw counts,
seeds, and manifest-order conventions are recorded in the repository's
Methods document and configuration export.

\paragraph{Sparse-stratum sensitivity.}
PlanBench's ring advantage comes from one passing repetition on the sole
task in its seven-object stratum. Stratified resampling always retains that
task, producing the degenerate $[0.25,0.25]$-pp interval. An unstratified
paired-task sensitivity interval is $[0.00,0.75]$ pp. This does not establish
general communication superiority or zero population uncertainty.

\paragraph{Policy comparator sensitivity.}
The original ZebraLogic primary contrast is $+3.776$ pp, interval
$[1.302,6.510]$, against the calibration-selected fixed-width baseline,
averaging conditions and ceilings. Confirmation gives $+0.917$ pp, interval
$[-0.583,2.500]$, against communicating fixed four at the two higher ceilings.
These are different estimands and must not be pooled. An interval spanning
zero does not establish equivalence. Full configuration counts are provided
in the repository's evaluation-cell table.
